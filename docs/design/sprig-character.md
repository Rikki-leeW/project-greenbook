# Sprig character standard

**Status:** Approved and locked 19 September 2026  
**Role:** Sprig, The Garden Keeper  
**Product:** Garden of Mine

Sprig's approved identity is defined by the canonical portrait and character reference sheet stored with the application assets. New artwork must use those images as visual references. Do not recreate Sprig from a text description alone.

## Canonical assets

- `src/images/sprig/sprig-character-portrait-approved.png` — primary face, finish and personality reference.
- `src/images/sprig/sprig-character-reference-approved.png` — proportions, views, expressions, clothing, satchel, notebook and palette reference.

The portrait is authoritative when a generated view differs subtly in the face. The reference sheet is authoritative for full-body proportions, clothing construction and accessories.

## Fixed identity

- Warm peach natural skin; never green.
- Large, warm chestnut eyes.
- Soft rounded goblin jaw; never a square human jaw.
- Small rounded nose with rosy cheeks and nose.
- Light freckles.
- Gentle, expressive eyebrows; never severe adult brows.
- Long pointed ears with a soft, friendly shape.
- Tousled chestnut hair.
- Distinctive layered green leaf cap with two upright leaves.
- Compact goblin proportions that feel capable rather than toddler-like.
- Warm, observant and quietly knowledgeable presence.
- Youthful-adult impression: neither a child nor an elderly or wizened figure.
- Adult-facing storybook watercolor and gouache finish with fine botanical detail.

## Core clothing and objects

- Moss-green, hand-stitched vest.
- Cream linen shirt with softly rolled sleeves.
- Earth-toned practical trousers.
- Worn brown garden boots.
- Weathered leather satchel.
- Brown garden notebook.
- Small botanical details may be integrated naturally into clothing and accessories.

Clothing can acquire believable garden wear, seasonal layers or task-specific additions, but the core silhouette, materials and palette must remain recognizable.

## Expression language

Sprig's expressions are subtle and calm. Approved foundations are neutral, curious, thoughtful and warm/happy. He may show concern, concentration, pride or gentle amusement when a scene requires it, but should not become manic, smug, mischievous, frightened or exaggeratedly cartoonish.

## Character purpose

Garden of Mine is the place and product. Sprig is its observant keeper. He keeps records, photographs and garden stories without pretending to know what the gardener has not recorded.

> You grow the garden. Sprig keeps the story.

Artwork should support atmosphere, orientation, emotional punctuation and empty states. Sprig must not crowd every page or become an interrupting assistant.

## Avoid

- Human or hobbit-in-costume appearance.
- Oversized conventional hat replacing the leaf cap.
- Green skin.
- Square jaw or severe eyebrows.
- Toddler proportions or childish presentation.
- Heavy ageing, deep wrinkles or a wizened appearance.
- Frightening, uncanny or hyper-real rendering.
- Glossy 3D styling.
- Unapproved costume or facial redesigns.
- Generating future Sprig artwork without the canonical source images.

## Production rule

Every new Sprig asset must be checked against the canonical portrait for face and personality, and against the reference sheet for silhouette, proportions, clothing and palette. Intentional departures require a documented design decision from the product owner.
