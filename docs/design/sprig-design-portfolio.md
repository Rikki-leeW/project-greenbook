## 2. Character essence

Sprig is a gentle garden keeper: warm, observant, quietly knowledgeable and pleased to help without becoming an intrusive assistant. He records the gardener's real work, photographs and stories. He never pretends to know something the gardener has not recorded.

> You grow the garden. Sprig keeps the story.

Sprig should feel trustworthy and companionable to an adult gardener. He is whimsical, but not childish, manic or toy-like.

## 3. Fixed facial identity

These traits must remain consistent:

- Warm peach, natural skin; never green.
- Large warm chestnut-brown eyes with soft highlights.
- Soft rounded goblin jaw; never a square human jaw.
- Small rounded nose with a rosy nose tip and cheeks.
- Light freckles across the cheeks and nose.
- Gentle, expressive eyebrows; never heavy or severe.
- Long pointed ears with soft, friendly shapes and warm pink inner tones.
- Tousled chestnut-brown hair with fine, irregular strands.
- Open, calm expression with a subtle smile.
- No beard, moustache, heavy wrinkles or aged/wizened features.

When likeness drifts, correct the eyes, jaw, nose, ears and hair against the approved portrait before adjusting secondary details.

## 4. Age, scale and proportions

Sprig has compact storybook goblin proportions. He appears youthful-adult and capable—not a toddler, baby, elderly gnome or human child.

- Head is moderately oversized for warmth and readability, but not chibi.
- Body is compact with believable shoulders, torso, arms and legs.
- Hands are small but anatomically useful and expressive.
- Boots feel sturdy and grounded.
- He may appear small within a garden, but should retain adult competence and confidence.

## 5. Signature silhouette

Sprig must remain recognisable at thumbnail size through:

- Long pointed ears.
- Tousled chestnut hair.
- Layered green leaf cap.
- Two upright young leaves at the crown.
- Moss-green vest over a cream shirt.
- Cross-body weathered leather satchel.
- Compact boots-and-trousers silhouette.

Do not replace the leaf cap with a conventional hat, hood or flower crown.

## 6. Core clothing and materials

- Moss-green, hand-stitched vest with subtle botanical wear.
- Cream linen shirt with softly rolled sleeves.
- Earth-toned practical trousers with gentle fabric texture.
- Worn brown garden boots.
- Weathered brown leather cross-body satchel.
- Brown garden notebook when the scene calls for record-keeping.
- Natural, tactile materials: linen, wool, aged leather, wood, paper and leaves.

Seasonal layers and task-specific gardening additions are allowed only when the core silhouette remains immediately recognisable.

## 7. Permanent clothing badge

Sprig's badge is now a fixed part of his standard outfit.

- Pinned naturally to the upper chest of his green vest.
- Small handmade rectangular tag, not a floating interface label.
- Pale cream card or light aged wood face.
- Simple dark green-brown outline or stitched/pinned edge.
- **“Sprig”** is the large, unmistakable first line.
- **“Garden Keeper”** appears beneath in smaller lettering.
- Lettering should be dark, clean and high contrast.
- Badge follows the vest's angle, perspective and lighting.
- It must not cover the satchel strap unnaturally.

Include the badge whenever Sprig's upper body is visible and the final scale is sufficient for it to read. At tiny icon scale, preserve the cream badge shape even if the secondary words cannot remain legible.

Never use the former detached gold oval or place a floating “Sprig — Garden Keeper” label on the page to identify him.

## 8. Approved expression language

Preferred expressions are subtle:

- Warm and welcoming.
- Quietly happy.
- Curious.
- Thoughtful.
- Calm concentration.
- Gentle concern.
- Modest pride.
- Soft amusement.

Avoid manic excitement, smugness, mischief, fear, exaggerated surprise, broad cartoon mugging or vacant doll-like expressions.

## 9. Gesture and posture

Sprig's gestures should feel useful and considerate:

- Open hand presenting the garden or an interface area.
- Holding a pot, seedling, notebook, photograph or garden tool.
- Kneeling to inspect a plant.
- Calmly observing, recording or tending.

His posture should be relaxed, balanced and capable. Avoid puppet-like limbs, stiff symmetry, action-hero poses or childish bouncing.

## 10. Illustration finish

The approved finish is an adult-facing storybook illustration combining watercolour and gouache qualities with finely rendered botanical detail.

- Softly painted surfaces with visible natural texture.
- Fine detail around the face, leaf cap, clothing and important plants.
- Gentle dimensionality without glossy 3D rendering.
- Natural material texture rather than plastic smoothness.
- Whimsical realism rather than anime, chibi, cartoon-vector or photoreal human styling.
- No watermark, signature or decorative text unless explicitly required.

## 11. Colour and lighting

The garden is led by rich, natural greens:

- Moss, olive, sage, herb and deep forest greens.
- Warm cream parchment and linen.
- Chestnut hair and weathered brown leather.
- Tomato red and vegetable colours as restrained accents.
- Soft peach skin and rosy cheeks.

Lighting should be soft neutral daylight with only a touch of warmth. Avoid heavy golden-hour beams, strong yellow casts, neon greens, cold blue grading or excessive contrast.

## 12. Depth of field

Use restrained cinematic depth of field:

- Sprig, his badge, his immediate activity and the primary interface/story area remain crisp.
- The nearest foreground leaves and vegetable-bed edges may be gently blurred.
- Distant cottage, fence and far garden soften into a calm background.
- Blur must create depth without making the garden indistinct or artificially smeared.

## 13. Flexible scene elements

The following may change when the story requires it:

- Pose and direction of gaze.
- Expression within the approved emotional range.
- Garden task or held object.
- Seasonal plants and subtle practical layers.
- Camera angle and framing.
- Garden location, provided it remains botanically rich and grounded.

These changes must not alter Sprig's identity, proportions, signature clothing, leaf cap, badge or emotional character.

## 14. Prohibited drift

Reject or regenerate artwork that introduces:

- Green skin.
- A human, hobbit, fairy-child or elderly gnome appearance.
- Toddler proportions or baby-like presentation.
- Square jaw, severe eyebrows or an adult-human facial structure.
- A conventional hat replacing the leaf cap.
- Missing crown leaves.
- Different hair colour or straight, tidy hair.
- Glossy 3D, plastic, anime or vector-cartoon styling.
- Heavy golden lighting.
- Missing clothing badge when it should be visible.
- Misspelled badge text.
- A floating identification badge or page label.
- Unapproved costume redesigns.
- Extra fingers, malformed hands, doubled accessories or inconsistent satchel straps.

## 15. Standard generation prompt

Use this as the starting prompt, then add only the required scene action and framing:

> Create Sprig, the approved Garden Keeper for Garden of Mine, using the attached canonical portrait for his exact face and personality, the attached character reference sheet for proportions and clothing, and the approved welcome artwork for his permanent clothing badge, palette and rendering finish. Preserve his warm peach skin, large chestnut eyes, soft rounded goblin jaw, small rosy nose and cheeks, light freckles, long friendly pointed ears, tousled chestnut hair, and layered green leaf cap with two upright leaves. He wears a moss-green hand-stitched vest, cream linen shirt with rolled sleeves, earth-toned practical trousers, worn brown garden boots, weathered leather satchel, and a pale handmade badge pinned to his upper vest reading “Sprig” with “Garden Keeper” beneath. He is youthful-adult, compact, capable, warm, observant and quietly knowledgeable—never toddler-like, elderly, manic or mischievous. Render in the approved adult-facing storybook watercolour-and-gouache style with fine botanical detail, rich natural greens, soft neutral daylight, only a touch of warmth, and restrained depth of field. Keep Sprig and his badge crisp. Do not redesign his face, silhouette, cap, clothing, badge or core palette.

Then append:

- **Scene/action:** what Sprig is doing.
- **Composition:** portrait, landscape, full body, close-up or interface placement.
- **Required objects:** only objects relevant to the scene.
- **Protected negative space:** where interface copy must remain clear.
- **Invariants:** everything that must remain unchanged.

- [ ] Eyes are warm chestnut and gently expressive.
- [ ] Ears, jaw, nose, freckles and hair remain consistent.
- [ ] Leaf cap and two crown leaves are correct.
- [ ] Proportions feel compact, adult and capable.
- [ ] Core vest, shirt, trousers, boots and satchel are recognisable.
- [ ] Clothing badge is present when visible and reads “Sprig”.
- [ ] “Garden Keeper” is correctly spelled beneath when scale permits.
- [ ] Expression is calm, warm and subtle.
- [ ] Watercolour/gouache storybook finish is preserved.
- [ ] Greens are rich and natural with only slight warmth.
- [ ] Foreground and distant background soften while Sprig remains crisp.
- [ ] Hands, straps, tools and anatomy are coherent.
