2. WHAT GARDEN OF MINE ACTUALLY IS

Garden of Mine began as a tool built by Rikki for her own gardening.

Gardening for her is immersive and meditative. She can spend hours absorbed in the garden, doing many little things, and later not remember precisely what happened.

The app exists partly so she can stay present while gardening while still creating a memory of what happened.

But the product has evolved considerably beyond a journal.

The fundamental proposition is:

Garden of Mine remembers what happened in your garden, connects those records together, and gradually helps you learn how your particular garden grows.

It is NOT primarily:

“What should I do with tomatoes?”

It is much more:

“What happened when I grew tomatoes before?”

and eventually:

“What appears to work in my garden?”

This distinction is strategically important if Garden of Mine eventually becomes an App Store product.
