============================================================
7. STRICT OWNERSHIP / NO DUPLICATION
============================================================

A major Sprig architectural rule:

INFORMATION HAS AN OWNER.

Relationships can gather that information elsewhere without copying it.

Examples:

Plant Story owns the plant's lived story.

Garden Event / Journal owns an event that happened.

Harvest owns an actual harvest.

Growing Recipe owns the recipe/setup.

Purchase owns the purchase.

Plan owns future intention.

Garden Note owns an unplaced thought.

Plant Reference owns reusable knowledge about a crop/variety.

Saved Tip/Source owns outside advice and provenance.

Garden Trial will own the PURPOSE AND THINKING OF A DELIBERATE EXPERIMENT.

Garden Almanac is DERIVED and owns none of those source records.

Do not duplicate the same information into multiple owners just because another screen wants to display it.

18. WHAT A GARDEN TRIAL IS
============================================================

Rikki experiments constantly.

Gardening is inherently trial and error for her.

However Garden Trials should NOT record every ordinary gardening variation.

A Garden Trial represents a DELIBERATE QUESTION.

The clearest distinction established:

Garden Note:

"I don't want to forget this thought."

Garden Trial:

"I made a deliberate gardening decision for a reason, and Future Me needs to remember what I was trying to discover."

A Trial is the place Rikki can move a whole thought-out experiment OUTSIDE HER HEAD and OUTSIDE transient ChatGPT conversations.

It remembers WHY she did something.

A Trial owns the experimental reasoning.

Potential lifecycle:

🌱 Start a Garden Trial

→ Why am I trying this?

→ What am I trying to find out?

→ What do I think might happen?

→ What am I deliberately changing/testing?

→ What am I keeping comparable / what are the groups?

→ Which existing Sprig records are participating?

→ What am I watching for?

→ Trial quietly unfolds

→ Trial-specific observations/learnings

→ linked real evidence accumulates

→ What did I learn?

→ Story gathered

22. TRIAL-SPECIFIC OBSERVATIONS
============================================================

Garden Trials may legitimately own their own small unfolding notes.

These are NOT ordinary garden history.

Example:

"This comparison is no longer completely fair because Bag 2 was knocked over."

That is context about the validity/interpretation of the experiment.

It does not necessarily belong in the Plant Story or Journal.

So a Trial can have Trial observations / learnings without becoming another Journal.

Distinguish:

REAL EVENT:
"Fed Bag 2 with PowerFeed."
→ Journal / Plant Story history.

TRIAL CONTEXT:
"Bag 2 received accidental extra shade for four days, so comparison is compromised."
→ Trial observation.
