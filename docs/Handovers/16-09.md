============================================================
1. PRODUCT IDENTITY
============================================================

Official product name:
GARDEN OF MINE

Sprig is NOT the product name anymore.

Sprig remains the:
- garden helper
- intelligence
- observational voice
- future advisory layer
- mascot/personality inside Garden of Mine

Do not rename internal repo/storage/database identifiers merely because visible branding changed. Internal "sprig" identifiers may remain where changing them provides no user benefit.

Core product idea:

Garden of Mine is not merely a gardening database.

It is a long-term garden memory system that turns the gardener's accumulated records into useful knowledge about their own garden.

Important positioning:

"Other apps know gardening. Garden of Mine learns your garden."

"Garden of Mine turns the story of your garden into knowledge about your garden."

The three conceptual layers are:

1. Gardening knowledge
   General horticultural knowledge.

2. Garden context
   What is happening in this gardener's actual garden.

3. Garden memory
   What the gardener's accumulated history reveals over time.

2. PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
============================================================

These are foundational rules, not decorative slogans.

"Purpose determines presentation. Relationships determine memory."

"No picker in Garden of Mine is ever final."

"Just one story, viewed from different places."

"Back remembers the journey. Home remembers where the record lives."

"Records own photographs. Relationships gather the story."

"Derive known context. Do not ask twice."

"Quick Add means quick journey, not cheap data."

Garden records own truth.

Intelligence is derived from those records.

Relationships should connect existing truth rather than create duplicate versions of it.

If the gardener can enter or choose something when creating a record, it should generally remain editable after saving unless it is deliberately immutable provenance.

Every relevant saved relationship should be reachable/clickable rather than displayed as dead text.

When displaying related records:
- Quick Peek where useful
- then allow navigation to the real detail record

Plans and actual records are conceptually different:

PLANS = future intention.
Plant Stories, Moments/Journal events, Harvests, Purchases, etc. = what actually exists or happened.

Completing a plan should create/prefill the appropriate real record and preserve provenance back to the plan. Do not mutate a plan into historical truth.

Natural language evidence may include:
- Journal/Moment titles
- Journal/Moment notes

- crop
- variety
- plant age
- dates
- seasons
- Growing Places
- Growing Recipes/setups
- Products/fertilisers
- Ingredients
- events/interventions
- harvest performance

Desired insight classes include:

Development/age patterns:
Example: repeated wilting or dieback around a similar plant age.

Seasonal recurrence:
Example: pests or mould recurring at a similar time/place/crop.

Performance/intervention patterns:
Example: lower harvests compared with comparable stories, while higher-performing stories share a fertiliser/setup/event.

Sprig must distinguish evidence/correlation from causation.

Use confidence-appropriate language:
- "I remember..."
- "I notice..."
- "You may want to watch..."
- "You may want to consider..."

Do not claim causal certainty without evidence.

5. FUTURE SPRIG ADVISORY / AI ARCHITECTURE
============================================================

Rikki wants eventual AI-enabled Sprig Advisory.

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE:

Sprig Intelligence must remain fully useful without AI.
Sprig Advisory extends it. It never replaces it.

"AI may consume Sprig Intelligence. AI must never become Sprig Intelligence."

Correct architecture:

records
    ↓
evidence / relationships
    ↓
derived context
    ↓
Sprig Intelligence
    ↓
optional Sprig Advisory

WRONG architecture:

records
    ↓
AI
    ↓
Sprig

Internet/provider/AI failure must leave local Sprig Intelligence useful.

AI suggestions or diagnoses must never silently become historical truth.

Provenance distinction:

"Garden of Mine knows..."
= the gardener recorded it.

"Sprig notices..."
= garden records support an observation/pattern.

"Sprig suggests..."
= broader horticultural reasoning supports an interpretation.

Future AI integration should:
- use a provider-independent service layer
- preserve privacy and consent
- consider cost/limits
- degrade gracefully
- not require an external AI app installed by the gardener

Do NOT rush an API integration.

Build the context/evidence plumbing first.

Likely first useful vertical slice:

Plant Story
→ Ask Sprig
→ question and/or photo
→ Garden of Mine supplies relevant context/evidence
→ Sprig Advisory reasons over it
→ response clearly distinguishes knowledge/evidence/observations
→ useful confirmed outcome can become a REAL garden record

Do not create a parallel chat database as the garden's historical truth.

Prefer contextual "Ask Sprig" from relevant records rather than beginning with a generic AI Assistant tab.

A future powerful behaviour:
Sprig may cautiously disagree with generic gardening advice when the gardener's own accumulated evidence supports a different pattern.
