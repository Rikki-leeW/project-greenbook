🌿 Sprig, the Garden Keeper

Status: 🌱 Seeded

Goal

Bring Sprig back into the garden as a gentle companion.

Sprig should never feel like an assistant or chatbot.

He is the quiet keeper of the notebook, appearing occasionally with encouragement, observations and gentle gardening wisdom.

Ideas
Reappear throughout the app, not only on the Welcome page.
Seasonal greetings.
Gentle empty-state messages.
Celebrate milestones naturally.
Offer occasional reflections from the garden.
React to the gardener's own history.
Appear visually on pages without becoming distracting.
Grow alongside the garden over time.

I love that because it isn't "Add mascot."

It's preserve the soul of the app.

And I honestly think that's exactly what this roadmap is for.

One more thought that ties everything together...

Your roadmap already asks:

Would another gardening app have done this?

Most gardening apps have databases.

Most have reminders.

Most have calendars.

Almost none have a gentle garden keeper who quietly grows with your garden over years. That feels wonderfully Sprig, and it's the sort of feature that people remember long after they've forgotten the list of functions. 🌿

The bigger thing is Sprig himself.

When you first imagined Sprig, he wasn't just a mascot.
He was the quiet old garden keeper.

And I think you've spotted it at exactly the right time.
I don't think Sprig should become Clippy.
He shouldn't pop up with speech bubbles every ten seconds.
That would completely break the peaceful feeling.
Instead, he should feel like someone who has quietly been walking the garden with you all along.
Sometimes he appears.
Sometimes he's only left a little note.
Sometimes he's just watching from the corner of a page.
